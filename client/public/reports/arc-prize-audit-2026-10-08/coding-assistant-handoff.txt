Matt—I’m a hobbyist, and ARC Explainer is a volunteer project I build in my own time, without earning anything from it. I built these features because I wanted to understand ARC and couldn’t find the tools I needed. I’ve offered to share the work with ARC Prize, and I’d be glad to see these capabilities become part of the official site. It’s been frustrating to see useful features remain missing after putting that work into making them available. These two asks point to existing implementations your coding assistant can build on.

Existing open questions: I asked months ago why publication of the raw Hugging Face results had stopped and was told I would receive an answer; I’m still waiting. I’m beginning to assume publication won’t resume, although that hasn’t been confirmed. If that is the decision, it makes incorporating these inspection features into ARC Prize even more important: community tools can’t show newer attempts and released reasoning without access to the records. I’ve also asked about Opus 5.5 ARC-3 results. The practical concern is preserving people’s ability to examine how models reach their answers.
