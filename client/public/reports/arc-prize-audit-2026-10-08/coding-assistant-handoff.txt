2. LET A RESULT OPEN THE ACTUAL ATTEMPT

Today:
- Task pages show whether a model passed and what it cost.
- They don’t show what the model drew or how it reasoned.

The ask:
- Clicking a result row opens the attempt: reasoning, submitted and expected grids, mismatches, cost and time.
- Filter to failed tasks. Compare two model versions on the same task.
- Show the source and model settings. Label anything unavailable.
- ARC Prize already holds these records from its own testing, so this doesn’t depend on Hugging Face or anyone else’s viewer.

Working example: https://arc.markbarney.net/task/13e47133?highlight=73845 (a failed Opus 4.6 run on 13e47133)
Current official page: https://arcprize.org/tasks/13e47133#model-performance
Check: a reader can follow a result to its actual attempt; source and settings are visible; unavailable fields are labeled.
