2. Make the underlying model attempt inspectable

Your task pages show whether a model passed and what it cost. They stop short of what the model actually drew and how it reasoned. ARC Explainer puts released reasoning, submitted and expected grids, mismatch highlighting and cost/time together for each attempt.

Requested change: let a result row open the actual attempt, with those fields together. Add filtering for failed tasks and comparison of two model versions on the same task when both records exist. Show the source and model settings, and label unavailable fields. You already hold these records from your own testing, so this wouldn’t depend on Hugging Face or anyone else’s viewer.

Implementation reference: one saved attempt (a failed Opus 4.6 run on 13e47133: its reasoning, both predicted grids beside the expected ones, and a mismatch toggle). Your counterpart: the 13e47133 result table.
