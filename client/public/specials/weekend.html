How to read it

A player is not a team

Players

A player is a Kaggle account, and it follows them everywhere. CPMP’s account is cpmpml, whichever contest or team he is in.

Teams

A team belongs to one contest, with its own name and roster. CPMP and Darragh Hanley are nvbanana in ARC-2 and two of the seven on NVARC3 in ARC-3. Giba plays ARC-2 on his own and joins NVARC3 for ARC-3. The head editor plays for ARC Mutants in ARC-2 and Team Turducken in ARC-3.

Rosters are locked

The team-merger deadline passed on October 26, so the teams you see now are the teams that finish.

Public and private

The board everyone watches is scored on a sample of the test data. The private board, revealed after the bell, decides the final places and the medals.

The clock

Monday, 11:59 pm UTC (6:59 pm Eastern). Submit early.
