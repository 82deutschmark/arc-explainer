How to read a team

Same player, different team

A team belongs to one contest. The same player can be on a different team in each, or on a team in one and alone in the other. CPMP and Darragh Hanley are nvbanana in ARC-2 and part of NVARC3 in ARC-3; Giba plays ARC-2 alone and ARC-3 with NVARC3; the head editor plays ARC Mutants in ARC-2 and Team Turducken in ARC-3.
