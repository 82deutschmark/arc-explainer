How it’s decided

Two picks, one private board

Pick two

Each team can select up to two final submissions. If a team doesn’t pick, Kaggle picks for it.

The private board

The private leaderboard decides the final standings. The public board everyone watches is scored on a sample of the test data, so places can move when the private scores arrive.

Rosters are locked

The entry and team-merger deadlines passed on October 26. The teams on the board now are the teams that finish.

Winners

Kaggle lists the winners announcement for December 4, and says the organizers reserve the right to update the timeline.

At stake

What’s on the line

ARC-2: eight progress prizes, from $75,000 for first down to $15,000 for eighth, a $275,000 Grand Prize for the best open-source solution writeup (the artifacts are due within seven days of the deadline), and a $150,000 bonus prize with its own conditions.

ARC-3: five final-leaderboard prizes, from $40,000 for first to $5,000 for fifth, and a $700,000 bonus that unlocks only if a team reaches 100% accuracy. Anyone eligible for a prize has to open-source their solution.

Medals

The gold line

ARC-2: 3,564 teams, so gold would run to place 17, silver to 178 and bronze to 356; ARC-3: 5,009 teams, so gold would run to place 20, silver to 250 and bronze to 500. Medals follow Kaggle’s standard bands for the final team count and are decided on the private board, not today’s public one.

What to watch Monday night

The last-day queue

Does a run sent before the bell but scored after it count? Players are asking. Kaggle’s staff have said only that the deadline and the submission process stay as they are.

The two picks

Which two submissions each top team chooses, and whether the choice holds up on the private board.

The swing

How far the private board moves the public order. CPMP says the swings will tell us how much the platform’s speed differences mattered.

The clock itself

Any change to the timeline would be posted by Kaggle on each contest’s overview page. We’ll flag it here the moment it appears.
