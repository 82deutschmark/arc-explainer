Team and thanks: versions to compare

The Tufa Labs and ARChitects thanks (public harness and public Milestone 2 notebook) stay as they are in every version. These versions replace the team section and the competitor, friend and ARC Prize thanks only. Nothing here is in the article yet.

As it read before Son's second review (8 Nov)

Our first leaderboard placement in September was just Son and Mark. Then the Milestone 2 notebook came out and the competition got fierce, so we decided to join forces with some other noble competitors. In October, Son's team merged with Keith Tyser's team, the ARC Slayers, and the combined crew took the name Turducken: a chicken inside a duck inside a turkey, and partly a tip of the hat to the Tufa team and Daniel Franzen, whose public work we build on.

Team Turducken is seven people, and none of this happens without every one of them: Son Pham, team lead. Son Pham and Slow∞Loop, harness iteration and inference optimization. Keith Tyser, reinforcement learning. Mark Barney, manager. Mark Barney, NT, ngyzly and Ronen Dunlea, game makers. Thank you all.

Thank you to Yi-Chia Chen, and to Sanbei and Pochi, the rabbits, for being such fierce and friendly competitors. Thank you to NVARC and XZ, just as fierce. Well played to both. Thank you to OpenMind for his friendship and encouragement along the way. Thank you to Simon Strandgaard, Dr. Tim Scarfe and the entire MLST community, and Jack Cole.

And last, a big thank you to the ARC Prize team, and to François Chollet and Mike Knoop, who created ARC and founded ARC Prize and gave all of us something worth chasing. Getting to attend the event in Boston was fantastic. Greg Kamradt, thank you for all that you do to keep the ARC Prize Foundation running; it doesn't run without you, and we're so grateful. A smaller but heartfelt shout-out to Permaximum, the Discord moderator, and to all the great community members on the ARC Discord server.
