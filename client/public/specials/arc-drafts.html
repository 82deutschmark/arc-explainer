Current essay direction · November 11, 2026

Intelligence, slop and creativity

Write simply about intelligence as the capacity to acquire capacity: learning how to do something you could not do before.

AI lets us make things we could not make before. But being able to produce something does not mean we have made something worth making. What makes the work creative rather than slop?

Personal experience can support the argument, but should not be the subject. Keep the focus on learning and creativity, not an autobiography.

This is the working brief, not a finished or approved essay. No saved draft below yet incorporates the latest direction in full.
