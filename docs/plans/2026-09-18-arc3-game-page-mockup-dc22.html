Walk a small green square to the yellow goal while pressing panel buttons that swing, flip, extend and slide pieces of floor into place. From level 5 you also drive a claw that carries floor for you. A press that pulls the floor out from under you costs 20 steps.

In plain English

You move the green square with the arrows, but only onto floor, and most of the path is missing. Buttons on the black panel change the floor pieces that match them. Build the path, walk it to the yellow square, and never press a button that removes the floor under you: that costs 20 steps and is undone.
